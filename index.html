One inbox for Instagram and WhatsApp orders, with AI that turns messages into paid orders

Aru Bakes is a sample home bakery in Astana. Its customers write in Instagram Direct and WhatsApp, in English, Russian and Kazakh. Try the flow below on the phone.

  1. Open Dana's Instagram message in the Inbox
  2. Check what the AI extracted and create the order
  3. Tap "Customer paid" to simulate a Kaspi payment
  4. Open Stock: the honey cake count went down by itself
  5. Open Today to see revenue and hours saved

Prototype for the AITU AI Startups registry, September 2026. Messages, customers and payments are made up; payment links are not real.
